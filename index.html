00 About

I am the Associate Dean for Media and Technology in the Center for Media & Technology Solutions at Johns Hopkins University.

My research explores how literary production changes over time and how new media shape that history. I combine archival research with digital methods to recover publishing histories and develop new ways of studying literary culture.
