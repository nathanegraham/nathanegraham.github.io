About

Builder, leader, scholar.

A builder-leader-scholar practice that moves between projects, writing, and music.

What anchors the work

The through-line is not a single medium or title. It is the design of systems: technical systems, organizational systems, publishing systems, and the smaller artifacts that make those larger systems legible.

That is why this archive holds institution-scale delivery, web tools, hardware projects, writing, and music in the same frame instead of splitting them into unrelated identities.

How the work connects

The practice moves between institutional leadership, practical building, writing, and music. Those modes inform one another rather than operating as separate professional identities.

The Work page uses three broad categories: Projects for things made, Writing for published thinking, and Music for recordings.

Current role

Institutional technology leadership

I work in academic technology and media systems leadership, translating technical change into service design, operational structure, and durable institutional capability.

Projects

Tools, hardware, and interface experiments

Smaller builds, prototypes, and interface studies keep the larger systems work grounded in materials, constraints, and implementation detail.

Creative practice

Writing and music

Digital Born gives the work a conceptual spine, while music with Dialect provides another way to make, collaborate, and publish.
